01

Rutgers Dining Hall App & Dining Revamp

A team project proposing a centralized dining app and a dining service revamp to help students access menu information, nutrition details, and meal swipe tools.

Project managementService designTeam project
Read project overview

The business question

How can Rutgers make dining information easier to access while using student feedback to improve the dining experience?

Project approach

Our team used an adaptive approach for the app concept, with planned features for weekly menus, nutrition and ingredients, meal swipe tracking, surveys, and meal swipe donations.

Scope and deliverables

The project centered on an app prototype, a dining revamp proposal, a feedback and analytics dashboard, and an implementation and evaluation report. Full university rollout, major renovations, and meal plan pricing were outside the project scope.

Learning focus

Connecting student needs with project scope, stakeholder coordination, and implementation planning.

02

TASA Organizational Analysis

A team analysis of the Rutgers Taiwanese American Student Association (TASA), examining how leadership turnover and informal working practices affect continuity and productivity.

Organizational behaviorInterviewsAnalysis
Read project overview

The business question

How can a student organization preserve knowledge between leadership teams and maintain effective working practices?

Project approach

The team gathered information through interviews and observation of a weekly event. The analysis connected findings to course concepts, including Expectancy Theory and diversity.

Key observations

Leadership changes created a risk of losing organizational knowledge. A highly social atmosphere also raised questions about how clearly responsibilities and performance expectations were communicated.

Learning focus

Using qualitative evidence to understand organizational challenges and connect them with management concepts.

Academic team project
